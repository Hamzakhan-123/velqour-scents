SHIPPING & POLICIES

Shipping

All Pakistan Delivery

We deliver VELQOUR SCENTS orders across Pakistan. Standard delivery charges are Rs. 250 per order.

Cash on Delivery

Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for eligible orders. A delivery charge of Rs. 250 is added to the order total.

Payment Methods

Checkout supports Cash on Delivery, Bank Transfer, Easypaisa, JazzCash and a Card Payment option. For Bank Transfer, Easypaisa or JazzCash payments, customers should retain their payment proof for order confirmation.

Returns & Exchanges

If your order arrives damaged or contains an incorrect item, please contact VELQOUR SCENTS promptly after delivery. Clear photo or video evidence may be requested so the issue can be reviewed and verified.

Privacy

Customer information is used for order processing, delivery and customer support. Customer information should not be published or shared publicly without appropriate permission.

Terms

Product availability, prices and promotions may change. The website should display the final payable amount, including applicable delivery charges, before an order is submitted.
